Who We Are

BAP Girls is a social club and hype team built around sisterhood, bonding, support, and having a good time on Second Life.

We love going out, partying, dancing, supporting events, creating memories, and bringing the energy wherever we go. But BAP is about more than simply showing up to a party.

We aim to provide women with a positive space to connect, build friendships, support one another, and enjoy their time on Second Life. We believe a strong community is created by women who communicate, participate, encourage each other, and genuinely want to see the group grow.

What We're Looking For

We Want Women Who...

  • Are positive, energetic, and fun to be around.
  • Are willing to contribute instead of remaining stagnant.
  • Communicate respectfully and respond in a timely manner.
  • Can work well with other members and leadership.
  • Are ready to participate and be part of the community.
  • Want to grow with BAP Girls and take on greater responsibility.
